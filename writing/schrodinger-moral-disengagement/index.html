Abstract

An essay on Bandura's mechanisms of moral disengagement and the role of willful ignorance: the argument is that deliberately avoiding knowledge of a harm does its moral work before euphemism, diffusion of responsibility, or dehumanization are ever needed, drawing on Milgram's obedience experiments and related work in moral psychology.
