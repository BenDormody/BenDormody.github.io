MSc Dissertation 2026 · 17 pp

Evaluating Football Player 1v1 Ability Using Elo Rating Models

Success-rate metrics treat every opponent as interchangeable — a winger who farms weak full-backs can out-rank one who beats elite defenders. This dissertation builds separate attacking and defensive Elo ratings for 1v1 duels from StatsBomb event data across the Premier League, La Liga, and Serie A, and shows where opposition-adjusted rankings diverge from raw success rate.
